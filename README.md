In recent years, the surge in digital text generation has increased the demand for intelligent document retrieval systems that go beyond lexical similarity and incorporate semantic and affective understanding. This research presents a novel framework for emotion-aware semantic document retrieval by integrating Sentence-BERT (S-BERT) embeddings with fine-grained emotion classification techniques. While traditional models like CNN, RNN, LSTM and CNN-LSTM have been effective in text modeling, they often fall short in capturing deep contextual semantics and emotional subtleties across long texts. Leveraging the transformer-based BERT and its sentence-level extension S-BERT, this work enhances retrieval performance by aligning both semantic similarity and emotional tone of documents. The system employs emotion classifiers trained on granular emotion labels to filter or re-rank results based on affective relevance. A comparative evaluation is conducted among S-BERT, CNN, RNN, LSTM, CNN-LSTM and vanilla BERT models on benchmark emotion-rich corpora to assess their effectiveness in both semantic retrieval and emotion recognition. Experimental results reveal that S-BERT significantly outperforms traditional sequence models in semantic retrieval tasks, while fine-grained emotion classification enhances the user relevance experience, especially in sentiment-sensitive domains like healthcare, education, and social media.
